Refund Policy

Last updated October 2026.

All Kashtro products are digital downloads. Please review product descriptions and previews carefully before purchasing. Refund requests are handled according to this policy and applicable consumer protection laws.

Digital products and refunds

Because our products are digital downloads, refunds are generally not available once the download link has been accessed. However, refund requests will be reviewed in accordance with this policy and applicable consumer protection laws.

When we can help

You can contact us if:

  • You were charged more than once for the same order.
  • Your downloaded file is corrupted or will not open.
  • You purchased the wrong product by mistake and have not accessed the download link.
  • A technical issue prevents you from receiving or using your purchase.

Please contact us within 7 days of purchase where possible, and include your order details. We will review the issue and may provide a replacement file, correction, or refund where appropriate or required by law.

Nothing in this policy limits any rights you may have under applicable law.

How to request help

Contact us through the Contact section of our homepage. Please include your order details and a description of the issue. We usually respond to inquiries as soon as possible. Thank you for your patience.

Payment disputes

If you experience a problem with an order, we encourage you to contact us first so we can try to resolve it. You may also contact your payment provider if needed. This does not limit your rights to dispute a transaction.
